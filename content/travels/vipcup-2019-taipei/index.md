Traveled to Taipei for ICIP 2019 as a finalist in the IEEE VIP CUP 2019.

Badges and hallway chats between conference sessions, a group photo with researchers from many countries, and after hours — walking out to glass towers with flags, a wall of colorful tiles, and one silly selfie with a glowing mascot wearing glasses that none of us can explain now. The conference banquet brought a stage of dancers in traditional dress in front of a giant ICIP sign, and one last team photo under a golden dragon carved into the ceiling.
