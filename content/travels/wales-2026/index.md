A green trail to Aber Falls led on to Llandudno's big limestone hill, the Great Orme — up its old tramway, along Marine Drive with the sea far below, a gull sitting on a wall above fields full of sheep, and a grinning pirate statue outside a fairground ride. Below, the old town buildings caught the evening light with the Orme's cliffs behind them, a church spire against the sky, and a day trip to walk around Conwy Castle.
