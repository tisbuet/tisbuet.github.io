Bangkok on foot — from the "Welcome to Thailand" sign at the airport, past tall buildings, to Wat Arun's tiled spire glowing in the afternoon sun, and Asiatique's riverfront lit up with string lights at night. Then boats hopping between limestone islands in Krabi's turquoise water, before slowing down in Phuket old town — pink and yellow shophouses, a yellow clock tower over a red crossing, and a shopfront buried in pink flowers.
